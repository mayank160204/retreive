# Vision — RETRIEVE

What problem are we solving?

Students preparing for high-stakes exams (MCAT) struggle to retain dense reading material. Silent reading alone produces poor long-term retention; learners need an active, repeatable practice loop that combines speaking, listening, and immediate feedback so they remember more and study more efficiently.

For whom?

- Pre-med students preparing for the MCAT who need a lightweight, low-cost practice tool.
- Busy learners who prefer short, focused practice sessions and immediate measurable progress.
- Tutors and study groups who want to assign and track spoken-reading practice.

Why now?

- Advances in speech recognition and streaming transcription make low-latency word-level feedback feasible.
- Students increasingly expect interactive, feedback-driven learning experiences (mobile-first, voice-enabled).
- Affordable cloud services (Deepgram, Firebase, Stripe) reduce time-to-market for an MVP.

Success metric

- Primary: 30-day active retention — at least 20% of new users return weekly for 4+ sessions within 30 days.
- Secondary: Per-session learning impact — average accuracy improvement (word-level) ≥ 15% after 3 sessions.
- Business: Conversion rate from free to paid tier ≥ 2% in first 90 days (after adding Stripe flow).
