# User Personas

## Rebecca — The Focused Finalist
- Age: 24
- Role: Full-time student, MCAT in 3 months
- Goals: Maximize retention, efficient study sessions
- Pain points: Limited study time, forgets dense passages
- Motivations: High score for med school admission

Rebecca uses short, 30–40 minute study blocks. She needs measurable progress and likes streaks and progress charts.

## Leo — The Busy Pre-Med
- Age: 27
- Role: Working professional studying evenings
- Goals: Make every session count, practice aloud to build fluency
- Pain points: Distractions, limited consecutive study days
- Motivations: Pass the MCAT while working

Leo prefers mobile sessions and values simple summaries and quick wins.

## Sonia — The Study Group Leader
- Age: 22
- Role: Peer tutor and study group organizer
- Goals: Assign passages, track group activity, celebrate badges
- Pain points: Coordinating practice and tracking group progress
- Motivations: Lead peers to better outcomes

Sonia will use leaderboards and badges to motivate her group.
